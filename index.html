ABOUT

A reproducible benchmark.

The benchmark is designed to separate pretrained representation quality from task-specific fine-tuning performance. Results will be added incrementally after the evaluation protocol for each benchmark is verified.

Dataset versions, metrics, model revisions, and evaluation settings will live in the benchmark data files alongside the reported scores.
